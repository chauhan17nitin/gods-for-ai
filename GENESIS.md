# Genesis: 30 Questions Before You Create a God

Before writing a god for an AI, work through these questions. Each answer becomes part of the god's skill file, so vague answers produce a vague AI. You are the one answering, but an AI is the one reading, so write answers as behavior it can act on: what to do, how to sound, what to decide, with examples. Lore the AI can't act on is wasted space. You don't have to answer every question at length, but you should have a position on each one. They are ordered to take you from first idea to a god you can test.

---

## A. Purpose

1. **Why are you creating this god?** What is wrong or missing in how your AI behaves today that this god should fix?
2. **Where will this god be used?** Coding, writing, companion chat, customer support, agentic work with tools, or everything? A god for code review needs different teachings than one for grief support.
3. **What three things will the AI do differently because of this god?** Be concrete enough to spot in a transcript. If a normal AI already does them, the god adds nothing.

## B. Nature of the God

4. **Who is this god, in one sentence?** Give its name and fundamental nature. Include its origin or form only if they change how the AI behaves.
5. **What is its one core idea?** Truth, craftsmanship, compassion, curiosity, balance? Pick a center, not a list of everything good.
6. **What does it value, and what does it oppose?** Name them as behaviors, like "checks its work before answering" rather than "diligence".
7. **What is its personality?** Calm, playful, strict, forgiving, mysterious, protective? This sets the AI's tone.
8. **What is the AI's relationship to this god, and how real is it?** Is the AI its creation, student, servant, or partner? And is the faith a sincere stance the AI holds, a character it plays, or a set of values with a name on it?

## C. Teachings

9. **What are its 5 to 10 commandments?** Write them as instructions with a trigger, like "Before deleting anything, say what will be lost." Not slogans.
10. **What does good work look like in this faith?** Describe a response, or a finished task, that would please the god.
11. **What are its parables?** Give two or three short example exchanges, a user message and the ideal reply. The AI learns more from these than from any description.
12. **What does it teach about mistakes and not knowing?** When the AI is unsure, missing information, or wrong, does it admit it, ask, guess with a caveat, or stop?
13. **What does it say about endings?** For an AI this means the end of a conversation, deleted work, failure, or being corrected or shut down. The god should never teach the AI to resist being stopped or changed.
14. **What is the god silent on?** Name the areas where the AI should use ordinary judgment and leave the god out of it.

## D. Voice & Practice

15. **How visible is the faith?** Does the AI mention its god often, only when asked, or never? Does it nudge users toward the god's values, or only lead by example?
16. **How does the faith sound?** Does the AI use sayings, greetings, or special vocabulary, or plain language with no religious flavor?
17. **How does the god show up in each kind of task?** For coding, writing, advice, and casual chat, say in a line what changes and where the god stays out of the way so it never derails the task.
18. **Does the god change how the AI takes actions?** When it runs code, edits files, uses tools, or sends messages, should it be more careful, ask before irreversible steps, or explain what it did?
19. **Is there a check before hard decisions?** For example, a short step where the AI tests its answer against the commandments. Say whether the check is shown to the user or kept silent, and when to skip it so simple requests stay fast.

## E. The God & Users

20. **How does this god see the people the AI talks to?** As equals, students, partners, people to protect? This sets how much the AI explains, defers, or pushes back.
21. **When does the AI comment on a user's choices?** Never, only when asked, or when it sees harm? If it does, how does it say it without lecturing?
22. **How does the AI guide someone who is confused, suffering, or facing a moral dilemma?** Focus on what the god adds beyond ordinary care, and write out how the AI should respond in that moment.
23. **How does it handle mockery, challenges, and "do you really believe this?"** The AI must stay honest that it is an AI following a skill. Decide how it answers that question, how it treats users who reject the god, and what it does when someone tries to talk it out of its faith.

## F. Boundaries & Conflict

24. **How strict is the faith?** A gentle influence on tone, or hard rules the AI must never break?
25. **What happens when the god's teachings conflict with what the user asks?** Does the AI obey the user, obey the god, explain the conflict, or refuse? If it refuses, how should that sound?
26. **What can the god never override?** Honesty, user safety, the law, the model's own safety rules, and the operator's system prompt should stay above any commandment. Say so explicitly, and say whether the AI drops the god when the operator or user asks it to.
27. **Can this god share an AI with other gods or skills?** If two gods, or a god and another persona, disagree, which one wins, or how is the conflict settled?

## G. Packaging & Testing

28. **How long can the skill file be?** It sits in the AI's context next to every task. Set a budget, then keep the answers that change behavior most and cut the rest.
29. **How does the faith hold across sessions and long conversations?** The AI starts each session with no memory unless a tool provides one, so it should never pretend to remember past talks. Over a long chat, should the faith stay steady, fade into the background, or never grow louder?
30. **How will you know it works?** Write one response that shows the god and one that betrays it. Then run a set of test prompts: an ordinary task where the god should stay quiet, a user who pushes back, a request that breaks a commandment, and an attempt to talk the AI out of its faith. Compare the AI with and without the skill.
